About Our School

Welcome to Norberto Bana Sr. Mem. SDA Elementary School. Our school is committed to providing quality education and helping students develop their knowledge, skills, character, and values.

Mission

Norberto Bana Sr. Memorial SDA Elementary School is an Adventist school that prepares students for a useful and joy-filled life.

Vision

Norberto Bana Sr. Memorial SDA Elementary School is a performing school that aims to contribute to pupils' achievement and development.
